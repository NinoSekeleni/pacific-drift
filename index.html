    Pacific Drift

    You are alone in an outrigger canoe at Point Nemo, the place in the ocean farthest from any land. You have a compass, a radio and the sky, and somewhere out there is a shore.

    • W paddle
    • AD steer
    • E raise or lower the sail
    • R radio
    • L lantern
    • C star chart
    • N map
    • Mouse look around
    • M sound
    • Esc pause

    The sky follows your own clock, so it is day on the water when it is day where you are. The voyage runs in real time and continues while you are away. Leave the sail up and the canoe keeps sailing its heading, or lower it and the canoe drifts.

    In navigator mode the map shows only where you reckon you are from your compass and paddling, and currents quietly carry you off it. Your true course is revealed at landfall.
