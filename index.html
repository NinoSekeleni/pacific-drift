Pacific Drift

You are alone in an outrigger canoe at Point Nemo, the place in the ocean farthest from any land. You have a compass, a radio and the sky, and somewhere out there is a shore.

  • W paddle
  • AD steer
  • E raise or lower the sail
  • R radio
  • C star chart
  • Mouse look around
  • M sound
  • Esc pause

The voyage runs in real time and continues while you are away. Leave the sail up and the canoe keeps sailing its heading, or lower it and the canoe drifts.
