    How to play

    Drag to look around. Row, sail or drift toward land. Your compass, radio and the sky will help you find your bearings.

    • W row
    • AD steer
    • E sail
    • R radio
    • L lantern
    • N map
    • C stars
    • J logbook
    • M sound
    • Esc pause

    Your voyage continues while you are away. Leave the sail up to hold your heading, or lower it to drift. Your progress stays in this browser.

    Live follows the boat’s real location and time. Experience uses your local clock with simulated weather. Explore any sky in the environment studio.

    Use a dead-reckoned position. Your true course is revealed at landfall.
