Fishing, launch and jellyfish

Tidewater by DRG Software Solutions LLC: procedural tackle, fish anatomy, boat geometry and fishing-fight logic, adapted for Pacific Drift's WebGL renderer. MIT licence.

Aurelia by Holtsetio: pulsing bell formula and inspiration for trailing jellyfish tentacles, adapted to a bounded CPU simulation. MIT licence.

Fishing recordings are CC0 field recordings bundled with Tidewater. Recording sources and processing.

The initial 16-species fishing catalogue uses broad simulated habitat envelopes, inferred coastal depths and estimated water temperature. It is not an exhaustive species survey or live fish-location feed. Visual anatomy uses procedural templates; estimated lengths and game rarity are not stock assessments.
